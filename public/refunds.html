Your right of withdrawal (EU and UK)

If you are a consumer in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you normally have a statutory right to withdraw from a distance purchase within 14 days, without giving a reason.

For digital content supplied immediately, that right can end once download begins — but only where you gave express consent to immediate supply and acknowledged that you would lose the right of withdrawal, and that acknowledgement was recorded in your order confirmation. If that did not happen, your statutory cancellation right remains, and you may exercise it by telling us clearly that you wish to cancel; you do not need to use any particular form of words.

Our own 14-day guarantee above applies either way. Whether or not the statutory right still applies to your purchase, we will refund you within 14 days if you ask. This policy is in addition to your legal rights, not a replacement for them, and nothing here limits any mandatory consumer protection in your country.

After 14 days

Requests made more than 14 days after purchase are considered case by case. If the app is genuinely faulty, will not run on a supported system, or is not as described, contact us and we will put it right — by fixing the problem or by refunding you. Your statutory rights in respect of faulty digital content are not limited to any time window we set here.

What happens to your licence

When a refund is issued, your licence to use Focus Terminal ends and we ask that you delete the app from your Macs. Focus Terminal has no account system and does not check licences over the internet, so this is a matter of good faith. Your flight history and preferences are stored only on your Mac and are never sent to us; deleting the app removes your access to it, and you can delete that data yourself at any time.

Duplicate or accidental purchases

If you bought Focus Terminal more than once by mistake, email us and we will refund the extra orders — no time limit applies to genuine duplicates.

Purchases made elsewhere
