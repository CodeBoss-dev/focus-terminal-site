Focus Terminal for macOS

Privacy Policy

Effective 11 July 2026 · Last updated 18 July 2026

The short version. The Focus Terminal app has no accounts and no servers of its own. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or use your personal data for tracking or advertising. Everything you do in the app stays on your Mac. If you request launch updates on this website, we use the email address you provide only for those updates.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the Focus Terminal macOS application (“Focus Terminal”, “the app”) and the launch-update form on this website, both published by Waaridh Borpujari (“we”, “us”). It describes what the app does and does not do with information on your device, and how we handle an email address you choose to submit on the website.

The app: no accounts, tracking, or data collection

  • Focus Terminal has no account system and no sign-in.
  • We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data, and we do not use it for tracking or advertising.
  • The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Website launch updates

If you enter your email address on the Focus Terminal website, the form is processed by FormSubmit and emailed to us. We use it only to contact you about Focus Terminal availability and launch updates. It is not used for tracking or advertising and is not sold or shared by us. FormSubmit states that it retains form submissions for 30 days. You can ask us to delete your address or stop contacting you at any time by emailing waaridh@icloud.com.

What is stored on your Mac

Focus Terminal keeps your data locally on your device only:

  • Flight history and passport — your completed focus sessions, stamps, achievements, and statistics (stored with Apple’s on-device SwiftData).
  • Settings and preferences — your home airport, cabin-audio choices, the name of your optional Focus Shortcut, and your Cabin Mode “no-fly” list (stored in the app’s local preferences).

This information never leaves your Mac through Focus Terminal. It remains in Focus Terminal’s local macOS container until you remove that container data. Deleting only the application from the Applications folder may not remove its container. To erase all Focus Terminal data, quit the app, remove the folder at ~/Library/Containers/com.waaridh.FocusTerminal, and empty the Trash. Backups you control, such as Time Machine backups, may retain earlier copies until you remove or expire those backups.

Maps

Focus Terminal displays flight routes and scenery using Apple MapKit. Rendering maps may require network requests to Apple to fetch map and imagery tiles. Those requests are handled by Apple and governed by Apple’s privacy policy and services terms. Focus Terminal does not add tracking to these requests and does not receive your location or map activity back from Apple.

Sending feedback

If you choose Send Feedback in Settings, Focus Terminal opens your email app with a pre-filled draft. That draft contains only the Focus Terminal version, build number, and macOS version — nothing else. Nothing is sent unless you decide to send the email yourself, and you can add or remove anything before sending.

Notifications

Focus Terminal uses local notifications for turbulence reminders and landing alerts only after you grant notification permission when macOS asks at boarding. These notifications are generated on your device. You can change or revoke this permission at any time in System Settings › Notifications.

Files you choose

Focus Terminal only accesses files and apps that you select through the standard macOS open and save panels — for example, saving an arrival-card image, or picking an app to add to your Cabin Mode no-fly list. It has no other file access.

Focus Shortcut and Cabin Mode

  • Focus Shortcut (optional). If you enter the name of a Shortcut you created, Focus Terminal asks macOS to run that Shortcut by name at boarding and landing. If you leave it blank, the Shortcuts app is never contacted.
  • Cabin Mode. While a focus session is in the air, Focus Terminal locally checks which app is currently in front only to compare it against your own no-fly list, so it can nudge you back. This check happens entirely on your Mac; the result is not stored or transmitted, and Focus Terminal cannot close or block other apps.

Cabin audio

The optional cabin-audio ambience is synthesized on your device and is off by default. Focus Terminal does not use the microphone and does not record any audio.

Children

Focus Terminal is a general-audience productivity app. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
