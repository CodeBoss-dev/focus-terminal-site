WindowSeat for iPhone and iPad

Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

Support

The short version. WindowSeat keeps your flight and focus data on your device. If you choose private iCloud sync, it can share history with the Focus Terminal Mac app through your Apple Account. The developer does not collect your flight data, use it for advertising, or track you.

What WindowSeat stores on your device

WindowSeat stores your home airport, tasks, flight history, passport stamps, achievements, and preferences locally. It uses Apple's SwiftData and app preferences. It does not create a WindowSeat account or ask for a password, name, or email address.

Optional private iCloud sync

If you choose Keep My Flights in iCloud, WindowSeat copies your flight history, passport stamps, achievements, home airport, and an app-generated account identifier to your private CloudKit database. This is the same private history used by Focus Terminal on a Mac signed into the same Apple Account. Apple manages your Apple Account; WindowSeat does not receive your account password, name, or email address. The developer cannot view your private database.

Sync is optional. WindowSeat keeps a local copy and remains usable when iCloud is unavailable. Stop Syncing keeps local and iCloud copies but stops future sync. Delete iCloud Copy removes the shared private CloudKit zone for both WindowSeat and Focus Terminal. Local copies on devices that already have them remain. Settings also lets you export a JSON copy of your data.

Optional device features

WindowSeat is the iPhone and iPad version of Focus Terminal, sold through the same universal-purchase App Store listing. Apple handles purchases; the developer receives no payment details. The app has no ads, in-app purchases, or tracking.

  • No-fly list: With your permission, Apple's Screen Time controls shield the apps and sites you select during a flight. Your selection stays on your device.
  • Arrival reminders: These are local notifications that you can enable or disable in Settings.
  • Cabin audio: Ambience is synthesized on your device. WindowSeat does not use the microphone or record sound.
  • Focus Shortcut: If you enter a Shortcut name, iOS opens Shortcuts to run it at boarding and foreground landing. It cannot launch Shortcuts while WindowSeat is in the background.

Apple Maps and Smart Analysis

Apple Maps may fetch map imagery from Apple to display the flight deck. On compatible devices, Smart Analysis uses Apple's on-device language model to summarize your locally stored statistics. WindowSeat does not send your history to the developer or an external AI service.

Feedback

Send Feedback opens a draft in your email app with only the WindowSeat version, build number, and iOS version prefilled. You choose whether to send it and what else to write. If you email the developer, the developer receives the content you send and uses it to respond to you.

Deleting your data

Deleting WindowSeat from your device removes its local app data. If you enabled sync, delete the shared iCloud copy separately in WindowSeat Settings before removing the app. Copies on other devices and backups may remain. Deleting the shared iCloud copy also affects Focus Terminal's synced history.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page and its update date will change. For privacy questions, email waaridh@icloud.com. For help using the app, visit WindowSeat Support.
