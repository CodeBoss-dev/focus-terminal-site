WindowSeat for iPhone and iPad

Support

Turn a focus session into a flight: book a route, board, watch the flight deck or cabin, and land with a record in your log.

Privacy Policy

Need a hand? Use Send Feedback in WindowSeat Settings or email waaridh@icloud.com. Include the WindowSeat and iOS versions when reporting a problem. The feedback draft does not include your tasks or flight history.

Getting started

Start a flight
Choose a home airport after the briefing. Pick a departure or search for a destination, then set your task and cabin class. Board from the printed pass. Your log, passport, and stats update after landing.
Flight Deck, Cabin View, and Pure Mode
Use the in-flight controls to switch between the live route map and cabin window. WindowSeat remembers your last scene choice. Pure Mode hides the extra instruments until you exit it. Use End Flight to save a partial session.
Share history with the Mac app
Choose Keep My Flights in iCloud during setup or enable sync later in Settings. Sign into the same Apple Account on your iPhone or iPad and Mac. WindowSeat and Focus Terminal share the private flight history; sync may take some time after landing.

Optional settings

No-fly list
Enable the no-fly list in Settings, allow Screen Time access, and choose apps or sites. WindowSeat shields your selection during a flight and clears shields at arrival or when you end the flight. Turning the feature off clears them immediately.
Focus Shortcut
Create a Shortcut in Apple's Shortcuts app and enter its exact name in WindowSeat Settings. iOS opens Shortcuts to run it at boarding and foreground landing. It cannot silently launch Shortcuts while WindowSeat is in the background.
Cabin audio and arrival reminders
These are separate opt-in controls in Settings. Cabin audio is synthesized on your device; arrival reminders use local notifications.
Smart Analysis
Stats works on every supported device. Smart Analysis requires iOS or iPadOS 26 or later, a device that supports Apple Intelligence, and an enabled, ready model. The Stats page explains when it is unavailable.
Your data
Settings lets you export your data, stop syncing, or delete the shared private iCloud copy. Deleting the iCloud copy also affects the Mac app's synced history, so export anything you want to keep first.

Troubleshooting

The flight map is blank
Check your internet connection and give Apple Maps a moment to fetch satellite tiles. Your flight timer continues while imagery loads.
iCloud history is missing
Check that both devices use the same Apple Account and that sync is enabled in each app. Open Settings and try syncing again. Your local history remains available while iCloud is offline.
Notifications or Screen Time shielding do not work
Check the corresponding iOS permission in Settings and the WindowSeat toggle. Both features are optional.

Requirements and privacy

WindowSeat supports iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later. The core flight flow works with local storage; internet access is needed for iCloud sync and Apple Maps imagery. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
